# Product research notes

## Patterns worth borrowing

### Source control from NotebookLM

NotebookLM lets users generate quizzes and flashcards from selected sources and tune topic, difficulty, and quantity. PrepCanvas should preserve this source-selection mental model while making readiness and next actions more explicit.

Reference: [Google announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/notebooklm-app-quizzes-flashcards/)

### Adaptive progression from Quizlet Learn

Quizlet Learn moves from easier recognition formats toward harder written recall and targets material the learner previously struggled with. PrepCanvas should vary task difficulty as evidence grows.

Reference: [Quizlet Learn](https://quizlet.com/gb/features/learn)

### Guided tutoring from Khanmigo

Khanmigo positions the model as a tutor rather than an answer engine. PrepCanvas's coaching loop should use explanations, probing questions, worked examples, and reflection instead of immediately revealing an answer.

Reference: [Khanmigo](https://www.khanacademy.org/khanmigo)

### Ordered study plans from StudyFetch

StudyFetch emphasizes turning materials into an ordered study plan and tracking what remains. PrepCanvas should always surface a small next action rather than a dashboard with no decision attached.

Reference: [StudyFetch support](https://www.studyfetch.com/support)

## Learning-science implications

Retrieval practice can produce stronger conceptual learning than repeated study, including on questions that require comprehension and inference. PrepCanvas therefore treats questions as a learning mechanism, not only an assessment mechanism.

Reference: [Karpicke and Blunt, 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/)

## Product opportunity

Many tools generate study artifacts. PrepCanvas's sharper opportunity is to connect four layers that are often separated:

1. source-grounded content;
2. a coaching strategy selected from diagnostic evidence;
3. topic-level mastery with evidence volume;
4. a transparent readiness signal and next best action.
